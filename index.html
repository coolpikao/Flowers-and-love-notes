My love,

I don't think love is about finding someone who's perfect. I think it's about finding someone whose imperfections you choose to love just as much as everything else. I don't just love your quirks — I adore them.

I never want you to question how much I love you. I want you to know that no matter what life brings us, I'll be right here beside you. Through sickness and health, through the happiest moments and the hardest times, I'll stay.

I want to be the reason you smile after a long day. I want to be the person you run to when you're excited, when you're scared, when you're overwhelmed, or when you just need someone to hold you close.

My goal isn't just to love you — it's to make you feel loved every single day. I want you to wake up knowing you're appreciated, respected, cared for, and cherished. I want to remind you how special you are.

If I give someone my heart, I want it to be completely yours. I want to celebrate every victory with you, hold your hand through every setback, wipe away your tears when I can, and never let you feel alone.

I know I won't always be perfect. I'll make mistakes, and there will be days when I fall short. But one thing I never want you to doubt is how deeply I love you. I'll always try to be better for you.

More than anything, I just want you to be happy. If I can spend my life making you smile, making you laugh, comforting you when you cry, and reminding you every day how much you mean to me, that will be more than enough.

I love you more than words could ever fully express, and no matter where life takes us, my heart will always choose you.
